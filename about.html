About · The Western Canada Institute

The Western Canada institute
of Supply Chain Canada.

Supply Chain Canada — West is the regional institute of Supply Chain Canada — the national association advancing the supply chain profession since 1919. We serve the procurement, logistics, operations and planning professionals of Alberta and British Columbia across three chapter councils — Calgary, Edmonton, and Vancouver.

Our Swim Lane
SCC West delivers regional events, chapter programming, and the Futures Conference. National designation programs (SCMP, SMT), policy and accreditation are stewarded by Supply Chain Canada nationally.
01 / Mandate

Why we exist.

Three commitments — to our members, to our region, and to the national association we sit within.

01

Advance the profession in the West.

Regional programming, workshops and conference content built around Western Canadian industry — energy, agriculture, mining, government, education, port logistics and Pacific gateway trade.

02

Convene the network.

Chapter mixers, the Awards Gala, and the flagship Futures Conference — the moments where the West's procurement leaders meet, in person, every year.

03

Support the national mission.

We promote the SCMP designation, the SMT program, and the standards Supply Chain Canada has set since 1919 — and deliver them to the West.
