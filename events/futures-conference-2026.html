About the Conference

More than a conference. It's a convening of the profession.

Hosted by Supply Chain Canada — West, the Futures Conference is Western Canada's premier professional forum for leaders in supply chain, procurement, logistics, and operations. At a time of unprecedented disruption, transformation, and opportunity, the event delivers the insight and practical intelligence professionals need to lead with confidence, credibility, and impact.

Focus Areas

Six forces shaping
supply chains today.

From geopolitical risk to digital transformation, the Futures Conference convenes the conversations that matter most to Western Canada's supply chain professionals.

Resilience & Risk Management

Building supply chains that withstand geopolitical shocks, climate disruption, and demand volatility.

Transportation, Logistics & Infrastructure

The networks, modes, and corridors moving goods across Western Canada and global markets.

Public & Private Sector Leadership

How governments, Crown corporations, and enterprises are aligning to strengthen Canada's supply chain.

Digital Transformation & Data

AI, visibility platforms, and predictive analytics turning data into decision advantage.

Sustainability & Transparency

Responsible sourcing, Scope-3 emissions, and the regulatory wave reshaping how we report.

Talent & The Future of the Profession

Attracting, retaining, and developing the next generation of supply chain leaders.
